# ART_DIRECTION.md — SKYLINE Placeholder / Design-Track Contract

**Status:** OPEN — detailed art direction is intentionally being developed in a separate design conversation/workstream.

This file exists so implementation has safe boundaries before the full Art Direction Bible is frozen.

## Locked visual/product constraints

- Mobile-first.
- Clean, recognizable, polished game-art finish.
- Relatively low production cost compared with feature-heavy Burst games.
- Tower tension is more important than environmental feature count.
- Official progression: Street → Skyline → Sky → Atmosphere → Space → Moon.
- Moon is reserved for 10,000x Max Win.
- Large outcomes must become visually more spectacular through height/world/camera, not new economic mechanics.
- Final submitted assets must be original; do not ship Stake Web SDK sample assets.

## Current environmental idea bank — not locked

Potential visual anchors discussed:

- Street: cars / urban life.
- Skyline: tall buildings / architecture.
- Sky: birds / open sky.
- Atmosphere: aircraft/upper-sky transition, deepening sky and scale shift.
- Space: stars / satellites / Earth curvature possibilities.
- Moon: iconic Max Win destination.

Exact object placement, palette, block material/style, perspective, lighting, VFX and logo treatment are pending the design track.

## Prototype art rule

Use deliberately neutral placeholders:

- simple block geometry;
- simple zone gradients/shapes;
- no production asset assumptions;
- no copyrighted/reference-game art.

Greybox motion must remain readable without final art.
